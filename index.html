Delete item?

This item will be permanently removed from the system on every floor. Any pending marks for it will also be cleared. This cannot be undone (you'll have ~10 seconds to undo from the toast).

Reset to defaults?

This will restore any built-in items that have gone missing from your inventory. Items you've deleted from the system stay deleted. Your custom items, edits, notes, and past order lists are not affected.

Settings

Data

Floor colors

Pick a color for each floor's top header. It applies subtly to the stock check screen so you can tell floors apart at a glance.

Dot color labels

Each color on the home screen floor dots can carry a custom label. Track progress however works for you — "Restocked," "Needs walk," "Fully done," whatever.

Reset stock selections?

All stock selections (OOS, LOW, STOCKED, OVER) and their quantities on this floor will be cleared. Items you added on the fly stay in place. Past order lists are not affected. You'll have ~10 seconds to undo from the toast.

Import backup?

Importing will replace all current data — inventory list, floor progress, past order lists, and settings — with the contents of the backup file. Consider exporting your current data first as a safety net.

Edit categories

Drag the handles to reorder. Tap a name to rename it. Renaming a category updates every item in that category.
